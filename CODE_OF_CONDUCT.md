# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to keep discussions and contributions respectful,
technical, and focused on improving a safe open-source project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
deliberate disclosure of private information, and attempts to bypass the
project's security boundary. Maintainers may remove content or decline
participation when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's configured
maintainer or security channel. Do not attach private ArcSuite material.
